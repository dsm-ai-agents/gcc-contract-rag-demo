# GCC Advisory & Client Engagements

Use this group for decisions made before or during the design, launch, expansion, or transformation of an India GCC. These agreements primarily govern the relationship between an international client and a GCC consulting firm.

[Return to the knowledge-base index](../index.md)

## Group scope

Included subjects:

- GCC feasibility and business-case development
- Indian city and location comparison
- Target operating model and governance
- Build-operate-transfer delivery
- Program-management office and implementation coordination
- GCC expansion, maturity and capability transformation

Usually excluded:

- Detailed incorporation filings
- Employee payroll or verification administration
- Office lease and hardware procurement terms
- Steady-state finance or procurement operations

## Distinguishing similar documents

- Use document 01 for designing a new GCC; use document 05 for changing an existing GCC.
- Use document 02 when the consulting firm temporarily operates the center and an eventual transfer is central.
- Use document 03 for coordination and governance of a multi-workstream implementation, not for the substantive vendor services themselves.
- Use document 04 when the central output is a location recommendation rather than a full establishment program.

## Example retrieval questions

- Which Indian city did the adviser recommend and what evaluation criteria applied?
- What deliverables form part of the GCC feasibility assessment?
- When must the BOT transfer-readiness assessment begin?
- Which employees, assets and records move during the transfer phase?
- Who owns the program risk register and how are delays escalated?
- What headcount and capability maturity outcomes are expected from the expansion?

# Entity Setup, Legal & Compliance

Use this group for forming and maintaining the Indian legal entity, obtaining registrations, managing corporate governance, supporting taxes, and documenting intercompany services.

[Return to the knowledge-base index](../index.md)

## Group scope

Included subjects:

- Indian private limited company incorporation
- Director and shareholder documentation
- Board, shareholder and statutory-register support
- GST, withholding and routine tax compliance coordination
- Establishment, labour and operating licences
- Intercompany service descriptions, cost allocations and cost-plus pricing

Usually excluded:

- Reserved legal opinions and court representation
- Independent audit and tax positions
- Recruitment and payroll processing
- Workspace, IT and facility operations

## Distinguishing similar documents

- Document 06 creates the entity; document 07 supports its continuing governance.
- Document 08 addresses taxes and tax registrations; document 09 addresses establishment, labour and operational registrations.
- Document 10 governs the economic relationship between the foreign group and Indian GCC; it does not replace statutory tax filings.

- Is bank-account opening an assured deliverable?

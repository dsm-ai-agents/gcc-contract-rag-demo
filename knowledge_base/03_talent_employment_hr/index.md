# Talent, Employment & HR Operations

Use this group for sourcing, engaging, employing, paying, administering, and verifying the workforce needed to build an India GCC.

[Return to the knowledge-base index](../index.md)

## Group scope

Included subjects:

- Recruitment process outsourcing and placement fees
- Temporary staffing and contract personnel
- Employer of Record arrangements before entity readiness
- Payroll input and benefits administration
- Candidate and employee background verification

Usually excluded:

- The GCC's target operating model
- Company incorporation and tax filings
- Office, equipment and technology support
- General finance and procurement operations

## Document selector

| ID | Agreement | Retrieve when the query concerns |
|---|---|---|
| GCC-DEMO-011 | [Recruitment Process Outsourcing Agreement](11_recruitment_process_outsourcing_agreement.md) | Hiring demand, recruiters, shortlist SLAs, success fees, candidate ownership or replacement |
| GCC-DEMO-012 | [Staffing and Contract Personnel Agreement](12_staffing_and_contract_personnel_agreement.md) | Temporary workers, rate cards, overtime, timesheets, statutory employer duties or conversion |
| GCC-DEMO-013 | [Employer of Record Services Agreement](13_employer_of_record_services_agreement.md) | Interim legal employment, payroll pre-funding, Client direction or migration to the Indian entity |
| GCC-DEMO-014 | [Payroll and Benefits Administration Agreement](14_payroll_and_benefits_administration_agreement.md) | Payroll calendars, input cut-offs, approvals, deductions, benefit enrolment or error correction |
| GCC-DEMO-015 | [Background Verification Services Agreement](15_background_verification_services_agreement.md) | Consent, check types, turnaround, adverse findings, retention or verification vendors |

## Distinguishing similar documents

- Document 11 locates permanent hires; document 12 supplies temporary personnel.
- Document 13 is the legal employer during an interim period; document 14 administers payroll without necessarily becoming the employer.
- Document 15 supplies verification results but does not make hiring or disciplinary decisions.

## Example retrieval questions

- What hiring volume and time-to-shortlist commitment apply?
- How are duplicate candidates and replacement hires treated?
- Who employs and supervises temporary personnel?
- What fee applies when a contractor converts to permanent employment?
- When must payroll funding and employee inputs be supplied?
- Who decides the consequence of an adverse background result?

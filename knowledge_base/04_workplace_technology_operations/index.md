# Workplace, Technology & Operational Vendors

Use this group for the physical office, lease and fit-out, end-user technology, IT service desk, facilities management, and physical security required by an India GCC.

[Return to the knowledge-base index](../index.md)

## Group scope

Included subjects:

- Managed seats and office amenities
- Lease, rent, deposit, fit-out and reinstatement
- Laptop, network and meeting-room equipment procurement
- IT incidents, device support and service levels
- Maintenance, reception, visitor control and physical security

Usually excluded:

- Entity incorporation and licences
- Recruitment, payroll and background verification
- Accounting and procurement operating processes
- GCC strategy and location study deliverables

## Document selector

| ID | Agreement | Retrieve when the query concerns |
|---|---|---|
| GCC-DEMO-016 | [Managed Workspace Agreement](16_managed_workspace_agreement.md) | Seats, shared or dedicated areas, amenities, utilities, lock-in or additional capacity |
| GCC-DEMO-017 | [Office Lease and Fit-Out Coordination Agreement](17_office_lease_and_fit_out_coordination_agreement.md) | Rent, security deposit, fit-out, possession, common-area charges or reinstatement |
| GCC-DEMO-018 | [IT Infrastructure Procurement and Deployment Agreement](18_it_infrastructure_procurement_and_deployment_agreement.md) | Bill of materials, equipment delivery, installation, acceptance, tagging, defects or warranty |
| GCC-DEMO-019 | [Managed IT Support and Service Desk Agreement](19_managed_it_support_and_service_desk_agreement.md) | Support hours, incident severity, response and resolution SLAs, access or device operations |
| GCC-DEMO-020 | [Facilities Management and Physical Security Agreement](20_facilities_management_and_physical_security_agreement.md) | Housekeeping, maintenance, reception, guards, visitor management, safety or facility incidents |

## Distinguishing similar documents

- Document 16 provides a serviced office; document 17 creates a property lease and fit-out obligations.
- Document 18 covers acquisition and initial deployment of technology; document 19 covers continuing support.
- Document 20 concerns building operations and physical security, not cybersecurity or application support.

## Example retrieval questions

- How many seats are included and how are additional seats priced?
- What is the lease lock-in and security-deposit treatment?
- When does rent commence relative to fit-out?
- What are the equipment acceptance and defective-unit remedies?
- What response time applies to a Severity 1 IT incident?
- Who controls visitors and responds to critical facility incidents?

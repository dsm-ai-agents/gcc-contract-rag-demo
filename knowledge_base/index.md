# GCC Contract Knowledge Base

> **Synthetic demo dataset.** All companies, individuals, addresses, transactions, and commercial terms in this knowledge base are fictitious. The documents are intended only for RAG, information-retrieval, classification, and demonstration use. They are not legal advice or executable contracts.

## Purpose

This knowledge base contains 25 agreements covering the lifecycle of establishing, building, operating, expanding, and transitioning a Global Capability Center (GCC) in India. Use this index as the first routing document. Select the most relevant knowledge group, read that group's `index.md`, and then retrieve the contract or clause that best matches the question.

## Retrieval sequence

1. Identify the GCC lifecycle stage and principal subject in the query.
2. Select one or, for cross-functional questions, two knowledge groups below.
3. Read the selected group-level `index.md` for document distinctions and retrieval cues.
4. Open the most relevant contract and locate the applicable heading or clause.
5. When answering, distinguish contractual facts from inference and identify the source document by `document_id` and title.
6. If two agreements overlap, compare their parties, dates, status, location, scope, and order-of-precedence provisions before selecting the controlling term.

## Cross-category routing

- **Entity incorporation followed by employee hiring:** start with Entity Setup, then consult Talent & HR.
- **BOT employee and asset transfer:** start with Advisory document 02, then consult Employer of Record, Workspace, IT Procurement, and Transition agreements.
- **New office launch:** consult Location Strategy, Entity Setup, Managed Workspace or Lease, IT Procurement, and Facilities Management.
- **Data or access during HR/IT operations:** retrieve the operational contract that creates the obligation; do not assume a standalone data-processing agreement exists in this dataset.
- **Vendor selection and later vendor performance:** consult Procurement and Vendor Management together with the specific technology, workspace, facilities, or HR vendor agreement.
- **Exit from a managed model:** start with GCC Transition and Exit Management, then inspect the termination, handover, employee, asset, data, and access clauses in the affected service agreements.

## Retrieval cautions

- Similar commercial concepts intentionally vary across documents. Do not generalize a fee, notice period, SLA, liability cap, or renewal term from one agreement to another.
- Treat YAML metadata as routing information. Treat the clauses within the selected contract as the source of contractual details.
- `status`, `effective_date`, `expiration_date`, jurisdiction, and contracting parties determine whether a document is relevant to a time- or entity-specific query.
- All content is synthetic and should not be used to make real legal, tax, employment, or regulatory decisions.

# Managed GCC Operations & Transformation

Use this group for steady-state cross-functional GCC operations, specialist finance or procurement services, performance analytics, and the controlled transition or exit from a managed model.

[Return to the knowledge-base index](../index.md)

## Group scope

Included subjects:

- Integrated multi-function managed services
- Finance and accounting operations
- Procurement and vendor governance
- GCC analytics, KPIs and management dashboards
- Transition, knowledge transfer, asset handover and access revocation

Usually excluded:

- Initial feasibility and location selection
- Entity incorporation
- Recruitment-only or Employer of Record arrangements
- Real-estate and hardware purchase terms

## Distinguishing similar documents

- Document 21 is broad and cross-functional; documents 22 through 24 provide deeper terms for specific operational domains.
- Document 23 governs procurement processes and vendor oversight, not the underlying vendor's performance obligations.
- Document 24 creates reporting and analytics obligations but depends on other systems and teams for source data.
- Document 25 governs an organized exit or handover and should be read with the affected service agreements.

## Example retrieval questions

- Which workstreams are included in the integrated monthly fee?
- Can the finance provider approve or release payments?
- When must the month-end reporting pack be delivered?
- When are competitive quotations required?
- Who owns source-data quality and dashboard refreshes?
- Which people, records, assets and access rights must be handled during exit?
